# B2 · Çıkarım modalları, ileri gelecek zamanlar, bağlaçlar ve deyimsel fiiller

## Çıkarım (deduction) modalları

Bir şeyin ne kadar **olası** olduğu hakkındaki tahmin.

| Kesinlik | Şimdi | Geçmiş |
|---|---|---|
| Kesin evet (%95) | He **must be** at home — the lights are on. | She **must have forgotten** — she didn't come. |
| Olası (%50) | It **might / may / could be** true. | They **might have missed** the bus. |
| Kesin hayır (%95) | That **can't be** Ahmet — he's in London. | He **can't / couldn't have seen** us — it was dark. |

**Geçmişte yapılmamış ama yapılması gereken:** You **should have told** me! *(Bana söylemeliydin.)* — I **shouldn't have eaten** so much.

**needn't have + V3:** You **needn't have bought** milk — we already had some. *(Almana gerek yoktu ama aldın.)*

## İleri gelecek zamanlar

**Future Continuous (will be + -ing):** Gelecekte belirli bir anda **devam ediyor olacak** eylem.

- This time tomorrow I**'ll be lying** on the beach.

**Future Perfect (will have + V3):** Gelecekte belirli bir zamana kadar **tamamlanmış olacak** eylem.

- By 2030, I **will have graduated**. *(2030'a kadar mezun olmuş olacağım.)*
- By the time you arrive, we**'ll have finished** dinner.

## Bağlaçlar ve bağlantı ifadeleri (linking words)

| İşlev | İfadeler | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **Ekleme** | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides, as well as | **Moreover**, the plan is cheap. |
| **Zıtlık** | however, nevertheless, although, even though, despite, in spite of, whereas, while | **Although** it was raining, we went out. |
| **Sebep** | because, since, as, due to, owing to | The match was cancelled **due to** the rain. |
| **Sonuç** | so, therefore, as a result, consequently, thus | He didn't study; **as a result**, he failed. |
| **Amaç** | to, in order to, so that | I saved money **so that** I could travel. |
| **Örnek** | for example, for instance, such as | Fruits **such as** apples and pears… |
| **Sonuçlandırma** | in conclusion, to sum up, overall | **To sum up**, the benefits outweigh the costs. |

> **although / despite farkı:** **Although** + cümle → *Although he was tired, he kept working.* **Despite / In spite of** + isim veya -ing → *Despite being tired, he kept working.* / *Despite the rain…*

## Deyimsel fiiller (phrasal verbs)

Fiil + edat/zarf birleşerek **yeni bir anlam** kazanır. Kelime havuzunun B2 bölümünde daha fazlası var.

| Phrasal verb | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| give up | vazgeçmek, bırakmak | I've **given up** sugar. |
| look after | bakmak, ilgilenmek | Can you **look after** my cat? |
| look for | aramak | I'm **looking for** my keys. |
| find out | öğrenmek, keşfetmek | I **found out** the truth. |
| turn on / off | açmak / kapatmak | **Turn off** the lights. |
| put off | ertelemek | Don't **put off** your homework. |
| run out of | tükenmek | We've **run out of** milk. |
| get on with | iyi geçinmek | I **get on** well **with** my boss. |
| carry on | devam etmek | **Carry on** working. |
| make up | uydurmak; barışmak | He **made up** an excuse. |
| take off | havalanmak; çıkarmak | The plane **took off**. **Take off** your shoes. |
| set up | kurmak | They **set up** a company. |

**Ayrılabilen phrasal verb'ler:** Nesne zamirse araya girmek **zorundadır:** Turn **it** off. ~~Turn off it.~~

## Vurgu ve devrik yapılar

- **Never have I seen** such a beautiful place. *(Hiç böyle güzel bir yer görmemiştim.)*
- **Not only** is he clever, **but** he is **also** kind.
- **What I need is** a long holiday. *(Benim ihtiyacım olan şey…)*
- **It was** Ali **who** broke the window. *(Camı kıran Ali'ydi.)*
