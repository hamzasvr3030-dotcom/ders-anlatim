# A2 · Gelecek zaman ve karşılaştırma

## Gelecek zaman

### be going to

**Önceden verilmiş kararlar ve planlar** ile **kanıta dayalı tahminler**:

- I**'m going to** visit my grandmother this weekend. *(Planladım.)*
- Look at those clouds! It**'s going to** rain. *(Kanıt var.)*
- **Are** you **going to** study tonight?

### will

**Anlık kararlar, sözler, teklifler** ve **görüşe dayalı tahminler**:

- The phone is ringing. — I**'ll** answer it! *(Anlık karar)*
- I **will** help you, I promise. *(Söz)*
- I think it **will** be a great match. *(Görüş)*
- I **won't** (= will not) be late.

### Present Continuous (kesinleşmiş planlar)

Saati, yeri belli **ayarlanmış** buluşma ve planlar:

- I**'m meeting** Ahmet at 6 tomorrow.
- We**'re flying** to Izmir on Friday.

## Sıfatlarda karşılaştırma

| Sıfat | Karşılaştırma (-er / more) | En üstünlük (-est / most) |
|---|---|---|
| Tek heceli: small | small**er** than | the small**est** |
| -e ile biten: large | large**r** | the large**st** |
| Sessiz-sesli-sessiz: big | bi**gg**er | the bi**gg**est |
| -y ile biten: happy | happ**ier** | the happ**iest** |
| İki ve daha fazla heceli: expensive | **more** expensive | **the most** expensive |
| **Düzensiz:** good | **better** | **the best** |
| bad | **worse** | **the worst** |
| far | **further / farther** | **the furthest** |

- Ankara is **bigger than** Konya.
- This book is **more interesting than** that one.
- Mount Everest is **the highest** mountain in the world.

**Eşitlik:** **as … as** → She is **as tall as** her brother. It isn't **as cold as** yesterday.

## Sayılabilen ve sayılamayan isimler

| | Sayılabilen (apple, book, car) | Sayılamayan (water, money, advice, information) |
|---|---|---|
| Çok | **many** apples | **much** water |
| Kaç tane / ne kadar | **How many** books? | **How much** money? |
| Az | **a few** friends *(birkaç)* | **a little** time *(biraz)* |
| Her ikisi | **a lot of / lots of** people, **a lot of** milk | |

- There are **a lot of** people here.
- I don't have **much** time.
- Can I have **a little** sugar?

> **advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, money** İngilizcede **sayılamayandır:** ~~an advice~~ → **some advice / a piece of advice**.

## Zarflar (-ly)

Sıfat **ismi**, zarf **fiili** niteler:

- She is a **careful** driver. → She drives **carefully**.
- quick → quick**ly**, easy → eas**ily**, good → **well** *(düzensiz)*, fast → **fast**, hard → **hard**
