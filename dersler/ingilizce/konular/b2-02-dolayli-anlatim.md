# B2 · Dolaylı anlatım ve ileri edilgen yapılar

## Dolaylı anlatım (Reported speech)

Birinin söylediğini aktarırken, aktarma fiili geçmiş zamandaysa (said, told) zaman genellikle **bir adım geriye kayar.**

| Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired." | She said (that) she **was** tired. |
| "I **work** here." | He said he **worked** there. |
| "I**'m watching** TV." | He said he **was watching** TV. |
| "I **saw** the film." | She said she **had seen** the film. |
| "I**'ve finished**." | He said he **had finished**. |
| "I **will** help you." | She said she **would** help me. |
| "I **can** swim." | He said he **could** swim. |
| "I **must** go." | She said she **had to** go. |

**Zaman ve yer ifadeleri de değişir:** now → then · today → that day · tomorrow → the next day / the following day · yesterday → the day before / the previous day · here → there · this → that · next week → the following week · ago → before

> Söylenen şey hâlâ doğruysa zaman kaydırılmayabilir: He said the Earth **is** round.

### say ve tell

- **say** (something): She **said** (that) she was busy.
- **tell** (somebody) something: She **told me** (that) she was busy. ~~She said me~~

### Dolaylı sorular

Soru düz cümle sırasına döner; yardımcı fiil (do/does/did) kalkar.

- "Where **do you live**?" → He asked me where **I lived**.
- "**Are** you hungry?" → She asked **if / whether** I **was** hungry.
- "What time **does** the train **leave**?" → I asked what time the train **left**.

**Kibar dolaylı sorular** (zaman kaymaz): Could you tell me **where the station is**? Do you know **what time it starts**?

### Emirler ve ricalar

**tell / ask + kişi + (not) to + fiil**

- "Close the door." → He **told me to close** the door.
- "Please don't be late." → She **asked us not to be** late.

### Diğer aktarma fiilleri

advise, warn, remind, suggest, promise, refuse, offer, admit, deny, explain, complain, insist

- He **promised to call** me. · She **suggested going** to the beach. · They **denied stealing** the money. · The doctor **advised me to rest**.

## İleri edilgen yapılar

### Söylenti, inanış (It is said… / He is said to…)

- People say that he is very rich. → **It is said that** he is very rich. → He **is said to be** very rich.
- They believe the painting was stolen. → The painting **is believed to have been stolen**.

### have / get something done (bir işi yaptırmak)

Eylemi **başkasına yaptırdığımızda:** **have / get + nesne + V3**

- I **had my hair cut** yesterday. *(Saçımı kestirdim.)*
- We're **getting our car repaired**. *(Arabamızı tamir ettiriyoruz.)*
- She **had her bag stolen** on the bus. *(Başına gelen kötü bir olay)*

### Edilgen + modal / infinitive / gerund

- The work **should have been finished** yesterday.
- I don't like **being told** what to do.
- She expects **to be promoted**.
