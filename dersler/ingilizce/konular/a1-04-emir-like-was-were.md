# A1 · Emir kipi, I'd like, like + -ing, nesne zamirleri ve was/were

> Bu konular British Council / EAQUALS **Core Inventory for General English** A1 listesinde yer alır. Past Simple, going to ve karşılaştırma A1'in sonunda başlar; ayrıntıları A2 konularındadır.

## Emir kipi (Imperatives)

Fiilin **yalın** hâli kullanılır; özne yazılmaz.

- **Open** the window, please. *(Pencereyi aç lütfen.)*
- **Sit** down. **Listen** carefully.
- **Olumsuz:** **Don't** + fiil → **Don't** touch that! **Don't** be late.
- **Öneri:** **Let's** go! *(Hadi gidelim!)* · **Let's not** wait.

> Kibarlık için **please** ekle: *Please close the door.* Daha da kibarı: *Could you close the door, please?*

## I'd like (kibar istek)

**I would like** = **I'd like** *(istiyorum – kibar)*. **want**'tan daha kibardır.

- **I'd like** a coffee, please.
- **I'd like to** book a table. *(Masa ayırtmak istiyorum.)*
- **Would you like** some tea? — Yes, please. / No, thank you.
- **What would you like?** *(Ne arzu edersiniz?)*

> **like** ≠ **would like:** *I **like** coffee.* (Genel olarak severim.) — *I**'d like** a coffee.* (Şimdi bir kahve istiyorum.)

## like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing

Sevme–sevmeme fiillerinden sonra fiil genellikle **-ing** alır:

- I **love swimming**. · She **likes reading** books. · He **hates getting up** early.
- **Do** you **like cooking**? — Yes, I do. / Not really.
- **don't mind** + -ing: I **don't mind waiting**. *(Beklemekten rahatsız olmam.)*

## Nesne zamirleri (object pronouns)

| Özne | I | you | he | she | it | we | they |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nesne** | **me** | **you** | **him** | **her** | **it** | **us** | **them** |

- Can you help **me**? *(Bana yardım edebilir misin?)*
- I love **her**. · Call **us** tomorrow. · I don't know **them**.
- Edatlardan sonra da nesne zamiri gelir: with **me**, for **him**, to **them**.

## was / were (to be'nin geçmişi)

| | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | I **was** at home. | It **wasn't** cold. | **Was** she happy? |
| you / we / they | We **were** tired. | They **weren't** here. | **Were** you at school? |

- Where **were** you yesterday? — I **was** at work.
- **There was** a problem. · **There were** many people.
- **I was born** in 2005. *(2005'te doğdum.)*

## can / can't / could / couldn't

- I **can** swim. *(Yüzebilirim.)* · I **can't** drive.
- I **could** read when I was five. *(Beş yaşındayken okuyabiliyordum.)*
- I **couldn't** sleep last night. *(Dün gece uyuyamadım.)*
- **Can / Could** you help me? *(Rica — could daha kibar.)*

## Çok temel pekiştiriciler

**very** (çok) · **really** (gerçekten) · **quite** (oldukça) · **a bit** (biraz)

- It's **very** cold today. · The film was **really** good. · I'm **a bit** tired.

- [ ] Saat, gün, tarih söylemek (at / on / in)
